The Reality

Your degree taught you to write code. The industry needs something else.

Walk into any software team today and AI-assisted coding is the default. The engineers you'll work alongside use Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and Antigravity to generate, review, and ship software at a pace that was impossible two years ago.

Your problem isn't that you can't code. You can probably already write Python or JavaScript. What nobody has taught you is workflow fluency — breaking a problem into tasks an AI can execute, catching what AI-generated code gets wrong, and working on a team where everyone is using AI at once.

Your college teaches you to write code. Your first job will expect you to orchestrate it — directing AI agents and automated pipelines, and owning what ships. That's the gap this program closes.

M1
Foundations — Get something live.
Month 1 · Weeks 1–4
Master the AI-first development loop: spec → prompt → generate → review → test → commit. Set up a real dev environment. Build your first full-stack app with a backend, a frontend that talks to it, and a database. By Week 4, your squad ships an MVP to a real URL and demos it to external guests.
W1 · First Ship W2 · Backend + Frontend W3 · Database + Persistence W4 · MVP Sprint
🎯 Deliverable: Working MVP deployed publicly + Code Walkthrough assessment
M2
Depth — Build at team scale.
Month 2 · Weeks 5–8
Add auth, integrations, and a test suite. Run real sprint ceremonies — standups, retros, PR reviews. Learn to work as an engineering team where everyone is using AI simultaneously. Month ends with an internal beta where squads try to break each other's apps.
W5 · Auth Sprint W6 · Data Modelling + Integrations W7 · Testing + CI W8 · Feature Sprint + Internal Beta
🎯 Deliverable: Feature-complete v1 + PR history review assessment
M3
Craft — Ship with confidence.
Month 3 · Weeks 9–12
Security audit, performance optimisation, refactoring AI-generated technical debt. Polish the product, write documentation someone else can actually follow, and prepare a real launch. Demo Day is public — industry professionals in the room.
W9 · Security Audit W10 · Performance + Polish W11 · Docs + Launch Prep W12 · Demo Day
🎯 Deliverable: Polished v1 live + portfolio case study

You write code, open PRs, attend standups, and work on tasks assigned by real engineering teams. Everything from months 1–3 gets stress-tested against real conditions.

FAQ

The practical stuff.

What students (and parents) ask us before applying.

Is this full-time? How many hours a week?
Yes — treat it as your full-time commitment for the semester. But very little of it is scheduled: the only fixed-time slots are mentor sessions, evaluations, and industry expert lectures. Everything else — building, sprints, squad work — you plan and do on your own time, the way real engineering teams work.
Will it clash with my college classes or exams?
Most colleges let students take a semester out for an industry program like this one. Check with your department before you apply — and if you need a letter or program details to share with your college, we're happy to provide them.
Is it online or in person?
100% online. You can join from anywhere. All you need is a laptop and a reliable internet connection.
Is the Month 4 internship guaranteed? Is it paid?
Yes, it's guaranteed — every student who completes Months 1–3 is placed in a one-month internship with a partner company. This internship is unpaid; it's part of the program, where you prove what you've built on a real codebase.
What happens after the program?
Students who complete the program successfully move into a 2-month paid internship with one of our hiring partners. Students who perform well in that internship receive a full-time offer.
Do I get a certificate?
Yes. You'll receive a certificate of completion — along with something more valuable to recruiters: a live product, a GitHub history of real PRs, and an internship on your CV.
What do I need to bring?
Your own laptop and a stable internet connection. Your Claude Code subscription is included in the fee for all 4 months.
Selection

We pick for potential. Not grades.

This is not a course you register for. It's a program you apply to. We cap each cohort at 30 students so every person gets real mentorship, real code reviews, and a team that pushes them.

We're looking for students who are hungry to build, honest about what they don't know, and ready to be uncomfortable in the best possible way.

💡
You don't need experience with AI tools. You need the mindset to learn to use them well. That's exactly what we're selecting for.

Drive to ship

🧠
Intellectual honesty
Can you tell us what you don't understand? The students who grow the most are the ones who know where their gaps are.
🤝
Teammate mentality
You'll work in squads. A student who makes the whole squad better is worth more than a brilliant solo builder.
⚙️
Basic coding foundation
At least one programming language. You don't need to be an expert — you need to be able to read and reason about code.

Curiosity about AI tools
