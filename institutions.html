For Institutions

Bring this to your engineering students.

Partner with us to offer AI-First Engineering as a co-curricular program for your 3rd and 4th year students. We handle curriculum, facilitation infrastructure, and AI coaching — you provide the students, the institutional context, and connections to local companies for the Month 4 internship.

The 4-month structure maps cleanly to an academic calendar: 3 months across a semester, 1 month internship over the break or into the following term.

  • 3 months experiential learning + 1 month structured internship
  • Lean staffing model — the AI Coach significantly reduces facilitator load
  • Students graduate with a live product, GitHub portfolio, and real internship on their CV
  • Industry mentors, Demo Day, and internship coordination included
  • Assessment framework with rubrics and walkthroughs built in
